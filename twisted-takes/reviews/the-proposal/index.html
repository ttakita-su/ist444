The Proposal Puts a Control Freak at the Mercy of Small-Town Alaska

The Proposal (2009)

Content advisory: Brief comic nudity, sexual jokes including a male strip routine, and some swearing.

Premise

Feared Manhattan book editor Margaret Tate is Canadian, and a visa mess could get her deported. Her fix: announce an engagement to her overworked assistant, Andrew, which means a weekend at his family’s home in Sitka, Alaska.

The Take

Watching someone who controls every room lose control of all of them is the whole joke, and a good one. At the office, Margaret holds the leash. Up north, Andrew’s family, his hometown and even the local wildlife take it.

What Works

Andrew’s family earns as many laughs as the leads. Betty White, as his grandmother, gets the strangest, funniest material, and the sunny harbor town makes the Paxtons feel lived-in. Dropped into that, Sandra Bullock commits to every humiliation, including one mortifying collision, while Ryan Reynolds answers so dryly that their insults bounce instead of sting.

What May Divide Viewers

The first half zips along on quick put-downs. Once the weekend turns to heartfelt family talks, the jokes thin out and the formula shows. If you came for bickering, you may miss the early snap.

Who Should Watch

Anyone who likes banter and doesn’t need a rom-com to surprise them. It’s also an easy family pick with teens, if a few PG-13 jokes won’t make anyone squirm.

Your take

Does the boss-assistant setup still play as harmless fun, or does the power gap between Margaret and Andrew get in your way?
