10 Things I Hate About You Is Smartest When Its Heroine Is Angriest

10 Things I Hate About You (1999)

Content advisory: Crude sex-related jokes, teen drinking at a house party and some drug-related scenes.

Premise

At a Seattle high school, new kid Cameron falls for Bianca Stratford, whose anxious dad has one rule: no dating until her older sister Kat does. Kat despises most of the school, so someone gets paid to ask her out.

The Take

The bones are Shakespeare’s The Taming of the Shrew, a play that treats a sharp-tongued woman as a problem to fix. This version flips the sympathy: Kat’s anger reads as intelligence, and the people irritated by her usually deserve it.

What Works

Everyone around Kat is a little ridiculous, on purpose: a dad who treats every date as a pregnancy scare, a guidance counselor (Allison Janney) drafting a steamy novel, and a castle-like campus scored with late-’90s guitar pop. Against that, Julia Stiles plays Kat bone-dry, so her put-downs land like verdicts. Heath Ledger plays Patrick loose and goofy; his bad-boy reputation never quite sticks.

What May Divide Viewers

Everything hinges on a guy taking cash to go out with a girl. Some viewers will shrug; others won’t laugh along. A few crude late-’90s jokes have aged badly, too.

Who Should Watch

Students who slogged through the play in English class, and viewers who want romance with more bite than sugar.

Your take

Does the movie truly celebrate Kat's refusal to play nice, or does it still quietly nudge her to soften up?
