What Jennifer Did Turns a Police Interview Room Into the Whole Story

What Jennifer Did (2024)

By Twisted Takes · October 18, 2026 · Spoiler-free

Content advisory: A real home-invasion shooting; long stretches of police interrogation footage.

Premise

According to the film, in 2010 a young woman called emergency services to report an armed break-in at her family’s home in Markham, Ontario. It follows the detectives on the case through their recorded interviews with her.

The Take

Most of the tension lives in one plain interview room. Built on police footage, the film turns pauses, follow-up questions and long silences into the main events. Gripping, yes, but told largely from the investigators’ side of the table.

What Works

The pacing is patient. Clips run long enough for you to form your own read before an interviewee weighs in, and at under 90 minutes it never drifts.

What May Divide Viewers

In April 2024, 404 Media wrote that the film uses what it called clearly AI-generated images of Jennifer Pan. TODAY reported that executive producer Jeremy Grimaldi told the Toronto Star they are real photos of her, with the background anonymized to protect the source. Worth knowing, since the film asks you to study faces.

Who Should Watch

Pick this if you would rather study an interrogation than watch a chase. It is rated TV-14, not TV-MA, but the subject is still heavy.

Your take

Is a story told mostly through police tapes and detectives' memories fair to everyone involved, or does it need voices from outside the investigation?
