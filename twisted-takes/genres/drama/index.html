Drama

Drama is where the stakes are ordinary and still enormous: a diagnosis, a first big role, a family deciding what to say out loud. No monsters, no magic, just people working out how to keep going, often with more laughs than you’d expect.

Our first Drama review is Don’t Say Good Luck, a Netflix coming-of-age story about a theater kid who lands the lead in her school musical just as her mom’s cancer comes back. It’s gentle, funny in the right places and honest about how life keeps happening around the hard parts. Keep tissues nearby. More family stories and coming-of-age films will join this page as the team writes them.
