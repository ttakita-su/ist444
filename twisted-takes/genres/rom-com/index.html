Genre hub

Rom-Com

A good rom-com runs on two things: a ridiculous setup, and two people who are much better at arguing than admitting anything. The fun is watching the plan fall apart.

Three of our launch picks lean on a scheme that should never work. 10 Things I Hate About You turns a strict high school dating rule into a Shakespeare remix with excellent comebacks. How to Lose a Guy in 10 Days sends a magazine assignment and a workplace bet crashing into the same relationship. The Proposal stages a fake engagement to dodge a visa problem, then ships everyone to Alaska. Crazy Rich Asians skips the scheme for something scarier: meeting your partner’s family, at an enormous scale. Choose by mood: snarky, chaotic, awkward or lavish.

Look for

  • Enemies to lovers
  • Fake relationship
  • Bets and schemes
  • Meet the family
  • Teen comedy
  • Workplace sparks
