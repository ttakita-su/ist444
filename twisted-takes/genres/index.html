Genres

Seven places to start. Each hub collects its reviews, the moods and subgenres to look for, and a question worth arguing about.

  • Sci-Fi

    Dream heists, black holes and alien grammar. Big ideas that still care about the people inside them.

    • Mind-bending
    • Space epic
    • First contact
    • Simulated worlds
    • Dream heist

    Includes: The Matrix · Inception · Interstellar · Arrival

  • True Crime

    Feature documentaries about real cases. We look at how each film tells its story, and how carefully.

    • Feature documentary
    • Online deception
    • Archival footage
    • Interrogation tapes
    • Viral fame

    Includes: American Murder: The Family Next Door · The Tinder Swindler · The Hatchet Wielding Hitchhiker · What Jennifer Did

  • Fantasy

    Long quests, magic schools and spirit bathhouses. Worlds built so well you want to move in.

    • Epic quest
    • Magic school
    • Spirit worlds
    • Curses and spells
    • Hand-drawn animation
    • Coming-of-age

    Includes: Hocus Pocus · Harry Potter and the Sorcerer's Stone · Spirited Away · The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring · Howl's Moving Castle

  • Rom-Com

    Bets, fake engagements and very loud families. Love stories where the banter does the heavy lifting.

    • Enemies to lovers
    • Fake relationship
    • Bets and schemes
    • Meet the family
    • Teen comedy
    • Workplace sparks

    Includes: 10 Things I Hate About You · How to Lose a Guy in 10 Days · The Proposal · Crazy Rich Asians

  • Horror

    Haunted houses, silent monsters and uneasy family visits. Fear that hits close to home.

    • Social thriller
    • Haunted house
    • Paranormal investigators
    • Creature survival
    • Family dread
    • Slow burn

    Includes: The Conjuring · Get Out · A Quiet Place · Hereditary

  • Drama

    School musicals, family secrets and growing up too fast. Real-life stakes, told with heart and a few laughs.

    • Coming-of-age
    • Family drama
    • Dramedy
    • Backstage stories
    • Inspired by real life

    Includes: Don't Say Good Luck

  • Comedy

    Satire, sitcoms and jokes with a point. Stories built to make you laugh first and think a beat later.

    • Satire
    • Adult animation
    • Sitcom
    • Political comedy
    • Internet culture

    Includes: South Park
