True Crime

Every film in this section is a feature documentary about a real case, with real people and real families behind it. That changes how we write about them. Our reviews look at how each film tells its story: the interviews, the archival footage, the pacing, and the choices about what to show and what to leave out.

The Tinder Swindler follows women who describe being deceived by a man they met on a dating app. American Murder: The Family Next Door is built almost entirely from material recorded at the time, from text messages to police video. The Hatchet Wielding Hitchhiker revisits a viral news moment and what followed it. What Jennifer Did leans heavily on police interrogation recordings. Four films, four very different ideas about how to handle a true story.

Look for

  • Feature documentary
  • Online deception
  • Archival footage
  • Interrogation tapes
  • Viral fame
