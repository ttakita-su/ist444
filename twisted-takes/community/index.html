Community

Twisted Takes is built for conversation. Here is this week's question, where to answer it, and the few ground rules that keep it fun.

This week's question · Week of October 19, 2026

Which launch movie would you hand to a friend who says they “don’t do” that genre?

Every genre has a gateway title, the one that wins over the skeptic. Pick one from our five launch genres and tell us who you would show it to and why.

Need a starting point? A Quiet Place, Spirited Away, The Tinder Swindler

Bring your answer to the Twisted Takes posts on Instagram, TikTok and YouTube, or talk it over with the people you watch with.

Ground rules

  1. Discuss the work. Talk about the story, the craft and how it made you feel. Critique the movie, not the person who liked it.
  2. Respect other viewers. Taste is personal. No insults, slurs, pile-ons or gatekeeping about who is a “real” fan.
  3. Label spoilers. Put “Spoilers for [title]” before anything about endings, twists or late reveals, and give newer releases extra room.
  4. Keep it relevant. Stay on the movie, show or question at hand. No spam, self-promotion or sharing anyone’s personal information.

True crime titles involve real people and real losses. Please keep those conversations especially respectful of victims and their families.

Where to find us
