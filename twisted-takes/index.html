Sci-Fi

Sci-Fi hub →

  • Sci-Fi · Simulated-reality action

    The Matrix

    1999 · Lana Wachowski and Lilly Wachowski

    Leather coats, green-tinted screens and slow-motion gunfights aren't decoration in The Matrix; they teach you how its world works.

    Sample review

  • Sci-Fi · Mind-bending heist

    Inception

    2010 · Christopher Nolan

    Christopher Nolan hands you a dream-heist rulebook in the first hour, then spends the second hour testing every rule at once.

    Sample review

  • Sci-Fi · Space exploration epic

    Interstellar

    2014 · Christopher Nolan

    The science talk gets heavy, but Interstellar's real engine is a father's promise to his kids, carried by a roaring pipe-organ score.

    Sample review

  • Sci-Fi · First-contact drama

    Arrival

    2016 · Denis Villeneuve

    Forget laser battles: Arrival makes vocabulary the stakes, as a linguist tries to talk with visitors whose writing looks nothing like ours.

    Sample review

True Crime

True Crime hub →

  • True Crime · Archive-only documentary

    American Murder: The Family Next Door

    2020 · Jenny Popplewell

    No narrator, no new interviews: this documentary rebuilds a Colorado missing-persons case from footage and messages recorded at the time.

    Sample review

  • True Crime · Romance scam documentary

    The Tinder Swindler

    2022 · Felicity Morris

    Rom-com gloss and real text threads let the women at its center show how, as they tell it, a jet-set dating fantasy became a money trap.

    Sample review

  • True Crime · Viral fame documentary

    The Hatchet Wielding Hitchhiker

    2023 · Colette Camden

    A viral 2013 news clip gets a second look in a documentary more interested in internet attention than in the meme itself.

    Sample review

  • True Crime · Police interview documentary

    What Jennifer Did

    2024 · Jenny Popplewell

    Built around police interview tapes, this documentary turns a 2010 Ontario home-invasion case into a tense, slow-burn procedural.

    Sample review

Fantasy

Fantasy hub →

  • Fantasy · Magic school adventure

    Harry Potter and the Sorcerer's Stone

    2001 · Chris Columbus

    Less a tight mystery than a guided tour of a magic school, with a stacked adult cast and sets that do much of the storytelling.

    Sample review

  • Fantasy · Spirit-world coming-of-age

    Spirited Away

    2001 · Hayao Miyazaki

    Miyazaki swaps swords for scrub brushes, building a coming-of-age fantasy out of chores, courtesy and quiet nerve.

    Sample review

  • Fantasy · Epic quest

    The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring

    2001 · Peter Jackson

    Peter Jackson turns sheer distance into the spectacle, and Howard Shore's music makes leaving the Shire ache.

    Sample review

  • Fantasy · Wartime fairy tale

    Howl's Moving Castle

    2004 · Hayao Miyazaki

    A tangled plot rides on gorgeous design: a wheezing walking castle, a blunt heroine and a waltz that makes housework feel romantic.

    Sample review

Rom-Com

Rom-Com hub →

  • Rom-Com · High school Shakespeare update

    10 Things I Hate About You

    1999 · Gil Junger

    A ’90s high school spin on Shakespeare that is sharpest whenever Julia Stiles' Kat gets to be furious and right at the same time.

    Sample review

  • Rom-Com · Double-con dating comedy

    How to Lose a Guy in 10 Days

    2003 · Donald Petrie

    Two schemers, one ten-day deadline: Kate Hudson and Matthew McConaughey turn a double con into a sparring match.

    Sample review

  • Rom-Com · Fake-engagement workplace comedy

    The Proposal

    2009 · Anne Fletcher

    A boss fakes an engagement to her assistant, and Sandra Bullock and Ryan Reynolds' prickly back-and-forth carries it all the way to Alaska.

    Sample review

  • Rom-Com · Meet-the-family fairy tale

    Crazy Rich Asians

    2018 · Jon M. Chu

    A lavish Singapore rom-com whose best scenes are Constance Wu and Michelle Yeoh sizing each other up across the family table.

    Sample review

Horror

Horror hub →

  • Horror · Haunted house

    The Conjuring

    2013 · James Wan

    Gliding camerawork, 1970s textures and a sincere investigator couple turn this haunted-farmhouse story into chills that build room by room.

    Sample review

  • Horror · Social horror thriller

    Get Out

    2017 · Jordan Peele

    Jordan Peele turns a meet-the-parents weekend into creeping social horror, where every friendly smile feels like a test.

    Sample review

  • Horror · Silent creature survival

    A Quiet Place

    2018 · John Krasinski

    A family living in silence on a remote farm makes every creak, footstep and breath feel dangerous in this lean, 90-minute creature thriller.

    Sample review

  • Horror · Grief-driven family horror

    Hereditary

    2018 · Ari Aster

    Toni Collette anchors a slow, punishing horror film about a family coming apart after a funeral, with unease that seeps in rather than jumps out.

    Sample review
