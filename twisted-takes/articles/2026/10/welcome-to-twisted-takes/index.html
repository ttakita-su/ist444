Welcome to Twisted Takes

Who we are, why a group of students started a movie and TV review community, and how you can join the conversation.

By Twisted Takes · October 17, 2026

Sample article. This is starter text written to launch Twisted Takes. The team has not yet reviewed it or approved it as its own opinion.

Twisted Takes is a movie and TV review community run by a team of students. You can also find us on Instagram, TikTok and YouTube, where we keep things quick. This blog is where we slow down: longer reviews, recommendations and questions worth arguing about.

Why we started

We share an interest in entertainment and, honestly, in the conversations it creates. Some of the best parts of watching something happen after the credits, when you find out a friend saw a completely different movie than you did. We want to help people decide what to watch next and give them a place to compare opinions, not just collect them.

Why “Twisted”?

Because a take gets more interesting when it bends a little. “Twisted” stands for different perspectives on the same title, and for recommendations you might not expect, like a horror pick for someone who swears they only watch rom-coms.

What you’ll find here

  • Reviews across five launch genres: Sci-Fi, True Crime, Fantasy, Rom-Com and Horror
  • Genre hubs for exploring one corner at a time
  • Watch Next collections that start from a film you liked and point you somewhere new
  • Discussion prompts at the end of reviews and posts

We’re starting with movies. TV reviews are planned.

A few ground rules

Every review is an opinion, not a verdict. If you disagree with us, good. Tell us why. Respectful disagreement is the point; personal attacks are not. We also label spoilers clearly, so you can read a review before you watch without anything getting ruined.

Join in

Start with the weekly question on the Community page. Follow along and jump into the comments on Instagram, TikTok and YouTube. And if there’s a movie or show you want us to cover, suggest it on any of our channels.

Curious about who’s behind the takes? Visit the About page.

Strong opinions. Better watchlists. Let’s get started.
