Comedy ages faster than any other genre and bites harder when it's good. The best of it is about something: a trend, a politician, the odd things people do online when they think nobody's watching. A great joke tells you something true before you've finished laughing.

We're starting with TV: South Park, now in its 29th season and still turning the week's news into an episode in about a week. Read our review of the season so far, then try our [guide to iconic South Park episodes]({{ '/articles/2026/10/iconic-south-park-episodes/' | relative_url }}) if you need a place to start. More comedies, on screens big and small, will join this page as the team writes them.
